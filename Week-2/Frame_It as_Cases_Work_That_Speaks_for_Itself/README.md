# Frame It as Cases: Work That Speaks for Itself

## Track

General AI Fluency

## Week

Week 2

## Objective

This assignment focuses on presenting projects as structured case studies instead of simple project descriptions. The objective is to explain the problem, the work completed, and the outcome for each project while maintaining a consistent personal voice. It also emphasizes writing authentic content that reflects real experiences rather than generic AI-generated language.

- Frame_It_As_Cases.pdf

## Outcome

Through this assignment, I learned how to present my technical projects in a more meaningful and structured way. I created case studies for my portfolio projects by describing the problem, what I did, and the outcome for each one. I also developed a personal voice card, wrote a short professional bio and contact section, and compared a generic AI-written paragraph with a revised version written in my own style. This assignment helped me improve the way I communicate my work and present my projects in a clear, honest, and professional manner.
