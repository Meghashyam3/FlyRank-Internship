## Track

General AI Fluency

## Objective

This assignment focuses on improving prompts through an iterative process by adding one improvement at a time. The goal is to understand how changes such as defining the goal, audience, context, output format, and constraints affect the quality of AI-generated responses while learning the fundamentals of prompt engineering.

## Outcome

Through this assignment, I learned that effective prompt engineering is an iterative process rather than writing one perfect prompt. By improving a weak prompt step by step, I observed how each change influenced the quality and relevance of the AI's responses. I also learned the importance of providing clear goals, audience, context, output structure, and constraints to receive responses that better match my learning requirements. This assignment helped me understand how small improvements in prompt design can significantly improve the usefulness and clarity of AI-generated outputs.
